Your data & backup

Transactions and budgets are kept in your private Google Sheet. Download a backup before major updates.

V3.1 backups include card checkbox flags and all category budgets. Restoring older backups defaults the card flags to No; older backups without budgets clear those rules.

Privacy & security

Your Google Sheet and Apps Script must stay private. Never publish your PIN, credentials or financial exports. Use GitHub Actions Secrets to change your PIN.
