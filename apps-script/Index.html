YOUR PERSONAL SETUP

Settings & backup

Manage budget, categories and your own data.

Monthly planning

Amounts apply to the month selected on the dashboard.

Budget compares only everyday/fixed expenses; investments and transfers stay separate.

Categories

Based on your Excel sheet. SIP is treated as investment; card repayments as transfers to avoid double-counting spending.

Your data & backup

Your records are stored in a private Google Sheet in your own Drive. Every change needs internet and is saved to Sheets. CSV/JSON are optional manual backups.

JSON backup keeps entries, custom categories and monthly budgets. Restore replaces current data after confirmation. CSV import adds transaction rows.

Privacy reminder

Source code may be public on GitHub, but this app and Google Sheet must be private. Google account sign-in and the server PIN are both required. Update your PIN through GitHub Actions Secrets and redeploy. Never commit your PIN or Google credentials.
